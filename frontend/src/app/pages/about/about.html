About SvenskBank

Our purpose and how the application is built.

Our purpose

SvenskBank is a small banking application built as a study project in cloud computing. Its purpose is to show how a real-world style application can be split into independent microservices that run on Kubernetes, scale on their own and keep their data safe in a persistent database.

What you can do

  • Open a new bank account with an optional first deposit
  • See all accounts and their balances
  • Deposit and withdraw money
  • Transfer money between two accounts
  • View the transaction history of an account

How it is built

MicroserviceTechnologyResponsibility
frontendAngular + nginxThe web pages you see, and forwarding API calls to the right service
account-serviceJava, Spring BootCreates accounts and keeps track of balances
transaction-serviceJava, Spring BootDeposits, withdrawals, transfers and history
databasePostgreSQLStores all data on persistent storage
